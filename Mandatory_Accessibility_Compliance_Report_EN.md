## Executive Summary

Korean law requires **7 mandatory ("의무") accessibility features** for a general office building of this size and floor area. Cross-referencing these legal requirements against the actual 28F construction documents produced the following result:

| Status | Count | Items |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Confirmed compliant** | 2 | Doors (main entrance & accessible toilet) / Accessible toilet (turning space, approach clearance, grab bar height, door width) |
| 🔴 **Compliance risk identified** | 2 (conditional) | Doors — several 800mm corridor doors below the 900mm minimum / Accessible toilet — room depth short of new-build standard |
| 🟡 **Unverifiable from available drawings** | 1 | Corridor clear width (dimension text unreadable at available image resolution) |
| ⬜ **Out of tenant scope / unverifiable** | 3 | Accessible route, disabled parking, main building entrance level difference, stairs/elevator (base-building items) |

**Bottom line:** Of the 7 legally mandated items, 2 are now fully verified against the drawings (with one flagged risk each), 1 remains unverifiable due to image resolution, and 3 fall outside the tenant fit-out scope (base-building/landlord responsibility).

## Part 1 — Legal Basis: What Is Mandatory and Why

### 1.1 Statutory Delegation Chain

Korea's accessibility requirements for this office are derived through a three-level delegation:

```
① Act on the Prohibition of Discrimination Against Persons with Disabilities — Enforcement Decree
   Article 12 (Content and Installation Standards of Reasonable Accommodation for Facilities)
   → "...shall follow Annex Table 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on Guarantee of
      Convenience Promotion for Persons with Disabilities, the Aged, Pregnant Women, etc."
        │
        ▼
② Act on Guarantee of Convenience Promotion for Persons with Disabilities, etc. — Enforcement Decree
   Article 4 (Types of Convenience Facilities) → Annex Table 2
   (Matrix: building type × 19 convenience-facility categories, marked Mandatory / Recommended / N/A)
        │
        ▼
③ Same Act — Enforcement Rule (Ministerial Rule)
   Article 2 → Annex Table 1 "Detailed Standards on Structure and Materials of Convenience Facilities"
   (Actual numeric dimension / structure / material specifications)
```

- Applicability threshold (Enforcement Decree Art. 11): buildings newly built, extended, or reconstructed on or after **April 11, 2009**.
- Annex Table 2 category applicable to this project: *"Financial institutions, offices, newspaper companies, officetels, and similar facilities (applicable only if floor area ≥ 500 m²)"* — i.e., **general office use**.
- **Applicability is based on floor area (m²), not headcount.** This project's 28F floor (≈1,760 m² / 18,947 SF) comfortably exceeds the 500 m² threshold.

### 1.2 Applicability Result for This Office Category

| Category | Convenience Facilities |
|---|---|
| **Mandatory (7)** | ① Access route · ② Accessible parking space · ③ Level-difference removal at main entrance · ④ Entrance door · ⑤ Corridor · ⑥ Stairs or elevator · ⑦ Accessible toilet (water closet) |
| Recommended (3 categories) | Toilet (urinal) · Toilet (washbasin) · Reception/service counter · Rest area for pregnant women |
| Not applicable | Bathing facilities, shower/changing rooms, tactile paving blocks, guidance/information systems, alarm/evacuation systems, guest rooms, spectator/reading seats, ticket booths/vending machines |

> General private-sector offices carry a lighter accessibility burden than government or public-institution buildings — several categories are merely "recommended" rather than mandatory.

## Part 2 — Detailed Legal Specifications for the 7 Mandatory Items

**Source:** Enforcement Rule of the Act on Guarantee of Convenience Promotion for Persons with Disabilities, the Aged, Pregnant Women, etc., Annex Table 1, "Detailed Standards on Structure and Materials of Convenience Facilities" (relating to Article 2, Paragraph 1).

> Note: figures below apply in principle to **new construction / extension / reconstruction**. Many items carry a relaxed standard for existing buildings, noted where applicable.

### ① Access Route

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Clear width | ≥ 1.2 m |
| Slope | ≤ 1/18 (relaxable to 1/12 where terrain makes 1/18 impractical) |
| Passing space | ≥ 1.5 m × 1.5 m every 50 m |
| Curb height | 6–15 cm |
| Surface | Non-slip, level finish |

### ② Accessible Parking Space

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Bay size (perpendicular) | ≥ 3.3 m (W) × 5.0 m (L) |
| Bay size (parallel) | ≥ 2.0 m (W) × 6.0 m (L) |
| Location | Closest bay to building entrance / vertical circulation |
| Access aisle width | ≥ 1.2 m |
| Floor slope | ≤ 1/50 |
| Floor marking | 1.3 m × 1.5 m |
| Bay stripe marking | 0.5 m × 0.58 m |
| Signage | 0.7 m × 0.6 m sign, mounted at 1.5 m height |

### ③ Level-Difference Removal at Main Entrance

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Threshold reduction | ≤ 2 cm between entrance and approach path |
| If difference > 2 cm | Ramp or wheelchair lift required (per respective detailed standards) |

### ④ Entrance Door

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Clear passage width | ≥ 0.9 m |
| Clear approach distance in front of door | ≥ 1.2 m |
| Side clearance (non-automatic doors) | ≥ 0.6 m |
| Floor threshold | None (≤ 2 cm if unavoidable) |
| Revolving doors | Prohibited |
| Door closer (hinged doors) | Closing time ≥ 3 seconds |
| Handle height | 0.8–0.9 m |
| Braille signage | **Mandatory** — mounted at 1.5 m height beside office/room entrance doors |
| Tactile paving | Installed 0.3 m in front of the main entrance |

### ⑤ Corridor

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Clear width | ≥ 1.2 m (≥ 1.5 m where a "living room"/occupied space adjoins) |
| Floor level difference | None (ramp if unavoidable) |
| Wall projections | ≤ 0.1 m protrusion between 0.6–2.1 m height (≤ 0.3 m for freestanding columns/stands) |
| Clear headroom | ≥ 2.1 m |
| Kick plate (below handrail) | 0.15–0.35 m |

### ⑥ Stairs or Elevator (at least one required)

**Stairs**

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Clear width | ≥ 1.2 m (≥ 0.9 m acceptable for outdoor egress stairs) |
| Tread depth | ≥ 0.28 m |
| Riser height | ≤ 0.18 m |
| Nosing projection | ≤ 3 cm |
| Handrails | Continuous, both sides |

**Elevator**

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Front approach/turning space | ≥ 1.4 m × 1.4 m |
| Interior clear floor area | ≥ 1.1 m (W) × 1.35 m (D) (**≥ 1.6 m width for new construction**) |
| Door clear width | ≥ 0.8 m (**≥ 0.9 m for new construction**) |
| Control panel height | 0.8–1.2 m |
| Additional features | Braille markings, audible signal device, floor-arrival indicator light required |
| Illumination | ≥ 150 lx |

> Note: Buildings ≥ 6 stories and ≥ 2,000 m² gross floor area carry an *additional* obligation to provide at least one accessible elevator, escalator, or wheelchair lift. Tower 2 (28 floors) falls under this rule — confirmation that the building's common elevators meet the accessible spec is recommended (though this is typically a base-building/landlord item, not part of tenant fit-out).

## Part 3 — Drawing Gap Analysis: Legal Requirements vs. As-Designed 28F

### 3.1 Scope Limitation Note

The project scope (per L.3 Project Information) is a **single-floor (28F) tenant fit-out**: wall/ceiling/floor finishes, interior and MEP design, fire-protection modification, and furniture. The building's ground-floor entrance, basement parking, and elevator core are **base-building elements owned and maintained by the landlord (PARC 1)**. As a result, 3 of the 7 mandatory items (①②③) are, in principle, **outside this tenant fit-out's scope** and cannot be verified from tenant-level drawings — including the previously-checked A.7 "B4F Plan" sheet, which turned out to show only a storage room detail, not the actual parking layout.

|---|---|---|---|---|

### 3.3 Detail: Accessible Toilet (Item 7)

The 28F floor plan (L.5) designates room **28-S07 "Gender Neutral"** as a unisex accessible toilet, adjacent to the standard Male WC (28-S22) and Female WC (28-S21). Its detailed dimensions are specified in sheet L.6, "Typical Gender Neutral Restroom" (item 8). Comparison against the legal standard:

| Sub-item | Legal Standard (new construction) | Legal Standard (existing-building relaxed) | As-Drawn Value | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overall room width | Reference only (clear area width ≥1.6 m) | — | **2,245 mm** | 🟢 Compliant, ample margin |
| Overall room depth | Clear area depth **≥ 2.0 m** | Depth **≥ 1.3 m** | **1,725 mm** | 🔴 **Short of new-build standard** (−275 mm) / 🟢 meets relaxed existing-building standard |
| Front turning space | ≥1.4 m × 1.4 m | Same | Turning circle **Ø1,500 mm** (encloses a 1.4×1.4 m square) | 🟢 Compliant |
| Side approach clearance | ≥0.75 m | Same | **750 mm** (matches grab-bar reach dimension) | 🟢 Compliant (exact minimum, no margin) |
| Horizontal grab bar height | 0.6–0.7 m | Same | **650 mm** | 🟢 Compliant |
| Vertical/secondary grab bar dimension | ≥0.9 m | Same | **900 mm** (as labeled) | 🟢 Compliant (label ambiguous — length vs. mounting height; recommend confirming) |
| Door clear width | ≥0.9 m | Same | **1,000 mm** — confirmed via Door Schedule D.1a, room 28-S07, Type S2 (single sliding door) | 🟢 Compliant |
| Entrance braille signage (1.5 m height) | Mandatory | Mandatory | Only a "braille sign for cold/hot water" beside the washbasin was found; no separate gender-neutral entrance sign identified | 🟡 Unverified |
| Fixture type (backrest-style) | Mandatory | Mandatory | Wall-mounted Western-style fixture confirmed in the drawing | 🟢 Presumed compliant |

**🔴 Key risk — room depth shortfall:** The room depth of **1,725 mm falls 275 mm short** of the 2.0 m new-construction standard, though it comfortably satisfies the 1.3 m existing-building relaxed standard. Since PARC 1 is an existing, previously-completed building and this is a tenant interior fit-out (not new construction), the relaxed standard likely applies — but this should be **confirmed with the local building permitting authority or the project's architect of record**, since the classification (interior renovation vs. "reconstruction") determines which standard governs.

**Reference — standard Male/Female WC:** Neither the Male WC (28-S22) nor Female WC (28-S21) contains a dedicated widened accessible stall; all wheelchair-accessible use is consolidated into the single unisex Gender Neutral room. This is legally permissible (the law requires at least one accessible water closet in the facility, not one per gendered restroom), and its location — centrally located near the core, close to the main hallway — is well-chosen. Whether a single accessible toilet is sufficient for the floor's occupant load (129 desks + additional seating) is a design/usability judgment call beyond the strict legal minimum.

### 3.4 Detail: Entrance Doors (Item 4) — New Finding

The full CD image set (found in the images shared earlier in this conversation) included **Door Schedule (D.1a)** and **Door Hardware Schedule (D.1a')** — not present in the original 8-page PDF excerpt. Cross-referencing opening sizes:

| Door / Room | Type | Opening Size (mm) | Legal Standard | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **28-S11 Entrance** (elevator lobby → office suite) | B2 (framed glass double door) | **1,800 × 2,700** | ≥ 900 mm | 🟢 Compliant (2× margin) |
| **28-S07 Gender Neutral** (accessible toilet) | S2 (single sliding door) | **1,000 × 2,700** | ≥ 900 mm | 🟢 Compliant |
| 28-S06a Hallway (lounge/corridor boundary) | S1 (multi-pocket sliding door) | 1,800 × 2,700 | ≥ 900 mm | 🟢 Compliant |
| 28-S06b / 28-S08a / 28-S08b Hallway (multiple corridor branch doors) | B4 (single framed glass door) | **800 × 2,700** | ≥ 900 mm | 🔴 **Below minimum** (effective clear width will be narrower still once door leaf thickness is deducted) |
| Male WC (28-S22) / Female WC (28-S21) entrances | — (no door) | N/A | N/A | 🟢 Open baffle-wall entry — no clear-width issue |

**🔴 New risk — 800 mm corridor doors:** Several hallway branch doors are scheduled at **800 mm**, below the 900 mm legal minimum. Whether this constitutes an actual violation depends entirely on **whether any of these doors sit on a required accessible route** (e.g., the only path to a workstation zone, meeting room, or the accessible toilet) versus serving a back-of-house space (storage, MDF room, etc.) where the requirement would not apply. **Recommend confirming each 800 mm door's location and function on the A.2/A.3 floor plans**, and whether an alternate ≥900 mm route (e.g., via the 1,800 mm sliding doors) exists.

**Conclusion:** Locating the full 104-image CD set shared earlier in this conversation — specifically the Door Schedule (D.1a) — allowed items ④ and ⑦ to be verified in detail. The main entrance (1,800 mm) and accessible-toilet door (1,000 mm) comfortably clear the legal minimum, but **several corridor branch doors at 800 mm fall short and require on-site/drawing confirmation of their role in the accessible route**. The accessible toilet is compliant on nearly every dimension, with one open risk: room depth (1,725 mm) falls short of the strict new-construction standard, though it clears the existing-building relaxed standard. The remaining gaps are (a) corridor width, blocked by image resolution rather than by an actual finding, and (b) three items that are landlord/base-building responsibilities outside this tenant fit-out's drawing scope.
